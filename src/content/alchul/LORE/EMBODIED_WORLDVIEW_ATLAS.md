# Embodied Worldview Atlas — Seeing the World from Within

**Status:** exploratory in-universe ethnography, not locked canon. Written 2026-11-09.
**Purpose:** establish a three-dimensional *lived* picture of the Alchul lineage—anatomy in motion, sensory world, interpersonal life, history as remembered from inside—and separate that from an outsider's natural-history description and from any claim to speak for Alchul.

**Authority:** `CANON.md` controls; `SPECIES.md`, `CIVILIZATION.md`, `ALCHUL.md`, `VISUAL_CANON.md`, `TIMELINE.md` and existing research constrain this working layer. Nothing here automatically canonizes a physiological mechanism, single religion, language, gender system, settlement name, or historical event.

## 1. Three distinct positions, not three omniscient narrators

**Outside investigator / biological view:** What can another species infer from anatomy, behavior, settlements, technical remains, and reproducible measurement? A factual observer can describe morphology and patterns, but cannot simply read subjective meaning from posture.

**Resident / emic view:** What is salient to a member of a given population, occupation, age and historical period? The 'world' is not a human-style panorama with additional superpowers. It is a moving set of sight lines, scent arrivals, substrate transmission, learned dangers, social obligations, and memories lodged in sites.

**Alchul-linked encounter / epistemic-limit view:** Occasionally, an impression appears to concern the *same place in another time or condition*. Residents may treat it as warning, contact, coincidence, inherited pattern, or a phenomenon not adequately named. We can portray the **experience and competing interpretations**; we **cannot** know Alchul's body, number, intention, timeline or interiority.

The third view must never be called a definitive “Alchul POV camera.” Even the most sensitive observer may be mistaken.

The current 3D console is illustrative, not a source of biological canon.

## 3. The resident's actual three-dimensional space

A human visitor's picture of a corridor is dominated by visible geometry: floor, walls, ceiling, door.

For a **provisional surface-era or first-below resident**, it may instead be read through linked channels:

1. **Sight:** motion, occlusion, silhouettes, the direction of another being's attention, light differences across surfaces, fine marks on pathways. Predatory heritage favors attention to movement and changing cover, but the exact visual field of evolved descendants is not reconstructed.
2. **Substrate:** foot and forelimb contact, tension through braced structures, resonance of steps and nearby flowing water. These give information about load and motion through *materials* rather than visual space alone. They are plausible sensory elaborations, not known maniraptoran receptor details.
3. **Air and chemistry:** odor, humidity, temperature, flow direction, smoke, contamination, fermentation, animal presence. A room has a changing chemical history; it does not merely contain furniture.
4. **Route and consequence:** does a path permit a loaded or egg-bearing body to turn? Can it be evacuated if water rises? Where does each pathway lead when blocked? A corridor is a relation among obligations.
5. **Social field:** posture, display, contact, rhythmic signals, distance, mutually known tasks, trained attention, boundaries of consent. Residents can recognize an approaching companion before either has a clear line of sight.
6. **Unresolved anomaly:** an impression of a place otherwise unavailable, sometimes temporally displaced. This is not a continuous aura filter or a sixth sense available to everyone at will.

**Caution:** these six channels are an analytical presentation, not necessarily their own sensory taxonomy.

### The Spring Margin, as an interpretive scene

A resident approaches a protected nursery shelf. The shelf has visual shape, but its meaning is not “a rock with two eggs.” Before entering, the resident checks whether a ventilation signal is stable, whether the surface beneath a step has changed, whether the chemical state of nearby cultures indicates contamination, whether a bearer can turn without twisting, whether the nursery attendant has issued a boundary signal, and whether a planned alternate exit remains accessible.

The nursery therefore occupies **physical space, sensory space, historical space, and social space** at once.

## 4. A body in motion rather than a posed specimen

**Founder-specific anatomy (canon / established reconstruction direction):** horizontal theropod balance; counterbalancing tail; digitigrade stride; head and neck mobile; forelimb grasping without primate thumbs; feathers and display; toothed head and specialized second pedal digit.

**Mature-population extrapolation (not locked):** predatory orientation survives in the habit of studying a path before committing mass and attention to it. They may use multi-contact postures for precision tasks, rest differently when carrying internally developing eggs, and coordinate movement in ways that place air, sensing surfaces, and escape lines before visual symmetry.

A doorway adequate for the agile specialist may be inadequate for another body type. Architectural dignity is measured partly by **who can pass, carry, rest, and leave**. This is an *ethical and engineering hypothesis*, not known universal law.

Don't encode their cultural evolution solely through a taller torso or larger cranium. Science, collective memory and adult education are not synonymous with a human-shaped head.

## 5. Biological life course: a provisional native-centered model

The only **locked reproductive stage** for peak surface forms is internal development through approximately 75% of the embryonic period, followed by laying for final external incubation. No sex categories, reproductive exclusivity, clutch size or membrane physiology are decided.

**Working life-course model:**

- **Preparation / lineage continuity:** not just gametes, but maintaining safe circumstances for egg-bearing parents, material stores, biological cultures and competent attendants.
- **Retention:** internal embryonic development shapes nutrition, oxygen demand, routes, work distribution, vulnerability and the rhythms of communal preparation.
- **External interval:** protective incubation becomes the joint responsibility of parent(s) and a trained group; number and duration remain unspecified.
- **Hatching and learning:** a young person's earliest distinctions might be felt from substrate, ventilation rhythm, body contact and caregivers before they understand those signals conceptually.
- **Juvenile learning:** observational and participatory apprenticeship means competence can be transmitted across non-genetic bonds.
- **Adulthood:** social recognition may depend on demonstrated reliability, expertise, care and risk-taking rather than a simplistic male warrior / female mother division.
- **Aging and death:** a body can die while routes, living cultures and stored observations retain consequences of its actions.

**Open social question:** Does genetic parenthood carry special status over the individuals who protect and teach a child? Cultures might differ. Avoid treating a single speculative kinship model as prehistoric fact.

## 6. How communication inhabits a room

Existing canon requires multimodal communication: vocal sound, posture, feather/filament display, chemical signs, contact, vibration, spatial arrangement, and later more direct group-linked cognition.

**Working translation protocol:** render their interactions in English but annotate which meaning comes from which channel. A brief turn of body, a cessation of vibration, or a barrier of held tail/forelimb may convey a meaningful refusal without sounding like a human sentence.

**Three provisional communicative layers:**

- **Signal:** the source of an observation (“felt in the shelf”, “smelled in intake air”, “seen at the threshold”).
- **Commitment:** what the communicator has agreed to do about it (“I will check”, “do not enter”, “duplicate this record”).
- **Uncertainty:** whether a report was witnessed now, inherited from teaching, reproduced experimentally, jointly perceived in altered state, or only suspected.

These labels are *writer-side translations*. A later linguistic reconstruction could derive native syntax instead of assigning English words.

An observer must not be able to make everyone believe an extraordinary perception solely through social rank. A society built on anomaly and inference has strong reasons to develop procedures for distinguishing observation from interpretation.

## 7. Kinship, belonging and practical ethics (contested proposals)

Their biological baseline is **facultative sociality**, not a permanent wolf pack. The Harvest increases tolerance and shared learning over deep time. By the peak surface era, complex alliances and institutions can exist without assuming they map to human marriage, household, state or priesthood.

**Proposed social unit:** a **care-and-competence circle** whose members may include an egg-bearing parent, genetic relatives, unrelated observers, food keepers, teachers and visiting specialists. Members do not have to reside together continuously. Membership is maintained by reciprocal responsibilities and repeated participation rather than presumed blood alone.

**Proposed normative tensions:**
- pursuit instinct versus not pursuing a companion;
- duty to secure young versus right of an egg-bearer to continue skilled work;
- privacy of sensation versus public safety when an observation concerns air/water;
- collective memory versus the right not to have an individual experience edited into consensus;
- the value of skilled risk-taking versus the cost imposed on others when it fails;
- a surface community's right to keep resources versus the needs of a permanent deep settlement.

None of these needs a universal moral solution. Different settlements and time periods can disagree intensely.

## 8. Daily time, art and memory

The human default of days, books, songs, and paintings should not automatically govern their cultural life.

**Working proposals:**
- A **time unit of returning condition**: wet-to-dry-to-wet, a familiar spring's chemical signature returning, a clutch's internal and external stages, a trained migration circuit. They still know astronomical cycles; this is not ignorance of days.
- A **multi-path record**: a learned walk through a settlement in which surface textures, resonances, chemical sequences and views combine to store technical instruction. It can be read only by someone physically engaging with the path.
- **Resonant art**: arranged strokes, timed contacts, pauses and material response; a work might be felt through the feet or forelimbs, not only heard.
- **Living archive**: a cultured organism encoding part of a method through controlled growth or chemistry, always supported by independent copies because living things fail.
- **Choreographed argument**: parties navigate the same environment following competing route predictions, making disagreement visible and testable rather than merely declaimed.

**Not assumed:** every settlement has living archive technology at the first below; all individuals can perceive every medium; a mnemonic path can preserve millions of years of history without loss.

## 9. Historical self-understanding — five possible transitions, not a single myth

A human historian might label their history by absolute geological dates. A surface or early subterranean resident may instead organize it around **changes in what a community can reliably keep**.

1. **The Returning Place:** ancestors noticed and revisited recurring resources. In later interpretations, the significance lies in returning even when hunger is absent.
2. **The Shared Taking:** the Harvest moves solitary or provisional aggregations toward learned tolerance, chemical craft and collective observation. Conflict and predation do not vanish.
3. **The Tested Trace:** their tools and practices become repeatable enough that a result can outlive one expert. A failed experiment is useful if the method can be remembered and corrected.
4. **The Second Breath:** living continuously where daylight does not reach requires reliable air, water, supplies and reproductive safety; the sky still feeds them.
5. **The Kept World:** much later, surface memories and materials are deliberately reproduced below, until the catastrophe turns dual-world living into deep-world survival.

These labels are **English historiographic placeholders**, not confirmed native names or era divisions. A present-day deep lineage might instead use geological cycles, an ancestral biological transition, or the founding of its own memory network as “year one.”

## 10. Two residents can inhabit the same place differently

**A surface-bred observer entering the First Below** may feel confinement, damp, costly air and a painful absence of distant moving prey. The safety of the place seems provisional. Their crucial landscape remains above.

**A young below-bred learner** may recognize air-route rhythms as familiarity, treat the bright entrance as dangerous distraction, and experience a failure of the artificial ventilation as more terrifying than darkness. The surface is vast but poorly bounded.

**A specialist encountering an Alchul-associated impression** may feel a known place become temporally ambiguous. Another specialist interprets the same experience as an unusual sensory prediction amplified by altered conditions. Their disagreement is culturally meaningful.

**A post-K–Pg descendant** may regard both early settlers as ancestors who lived under severe technological limitation. A current New Madrid citizen might preserve those memories while inhabiting a morphology, sensory environment and social structure no peak-era individual would recognize.

The point is not to declare one “their perspective”; it is to make **multiple, historically conditioned native perspectives** available.

## 11. What an Alchul lens can show without violating canon

An educational 3D experience might show **resident reports** of the same spring at two incompatible times, perhaps as transient superposed waterlines or delayed acoustic signatures. It must always indicate that these are **interpretive translations of testimony**. The engine must never display a definitive entity or claim, “This is what Alchul sees.”

An anomalous experience is not free proof of its cause. A useful scene includes at least three competing accounts:
- the observer's report;
- a sensory/technical explanation;
- the unresolved remainder that repeated measurements do not yet settle.

At most, this is a **lens on contact phenomena**. It is not an objective image of a being.

## 12. 3D story-camera checklist

Show the body **from beside, above, eye-level, and within the character's navigated space**. Each view answers a different question:

1. **Beside:** mass distribution, stride, forelimb reach, tail and head behavior—only the era-appropriate approved morphology.
2. **Above:** route choice, hunting terrain, water routes, shared access, nursery safety.
3. **At body height:** occlusion and the choice to approach, make contact, take a risk or withdraw.
4. **Tactile/sensory annotation:** substrate vibration, pressure, odor, airflow and learned landmarks shown as editorial overlays, **not supernatural neon radar**.
5. **Communal scene:** non-verbal cues and distributed knowledge, not all individuals assembled facing a leader.
6. **Contact testimony:** temporal juxtaposition with multiple interpretations and uncertainty plainly shown.

The camera may reveal information for an outsider that a resident cannot know, and vice versa. Label the difference.

## 13. Canon gate / open dossier

Before rendering a 'definitive' individual from an era, resolve: evolved anatomy for that era, respiration, sensory organs, gestation mechanics, range of body sizes and roles, language translation conventions, kinship norms, diet, distinctive age stages, and variation among communities.

**Keep open:** Alchul's ontology, true viewpoint, gender, goals, physical shape, whether each anomaly originates with Alchul, whether a cultural tradition sees it as benevolent, and any claim that all descendants share one religion.

**Research grounding:** Real animals demonstrate rich sensory integration and social transmission, and fossil maniraptoran anatomy constrains early body plans. These analogies do not prove the invented lineage's speculative sensory abilities. See:
- van Leeuwen et al. 2024, *Chimpanzees use social information to acquire a skill they fail to innovate*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-01836-5
- Morgan & Feldman 2025, *Human culture is uniquely open-ended rather than uniquely cumulative*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-02035-y
- Qin et al. 2023, *Functional space analyses reveal the function and evolution of the most bizarre theropod manual unguals*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-023-04552-4
- Existing Alchul `RESEARCH/FOUNDERS.md` and `RESEARCH/PEAK_SURFACE_CIVILIZATION.md`.

**Key rule:** build every tradition from a physiological need, an environmental recurring condition, or an observable anomaly; then allow generations to re-interpret it. They are people with disagreements, not a single species-wide mythology.
