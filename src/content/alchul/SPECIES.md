# Species — Founder Population

Status: provisional biological reconstruction. Canon may tighten as research improves.

## Working identity

Founder taxon: indeterminate Appalachian dromaeosaurid / maniraptoran population, northern Mississippi Embayment, approximately 80–66 Ma.

The founder population should not be assigned a formal modern species name yet.

## Overall appearance

The ancestral animal is a feathered terrestrial predator, not a bird and not a lizard-like movie raptor.

Provisional morphology:
- small-to-medium dromaeosaur-grade body
- long counterbalancing tail
- narrow toothed head
- large forward-facing eyes
- feathered torso and forelimbs
- three-fingered grasping hands with curved claws
- powerful digitigrade hindlimbs
- enlarged second-toe claw
- scaled lower legs and feet
- alert, lightweight, fast-moving build

## Physiology

Likely high activity level and elevated metabolism consistent with derived maniraptoran physiology.

Do not assume mammalian thermoregulation. A heterothermic endothermic model is plausible for close relatives.

## Cognition

The founder is not sapient.

Important baseline capacities:
- strong visual attention
- route and place memory
- prey prediction
- object investigation
- observational learning
- behavioral flexibility
- repeated use of successful tools/sites if reinforcement occurs
- individual recognition is plausible but not yet canonized

## Manipulation

Hands are already useful but not humanlike.

They can:
- seize prey
- hold and brace food
- pull vegetation or bark
- turn or displace small objects
- carry objects short distances
- perform repeated learned actions

Precision grip and true opposable-thumb function belong to later evolution.

## Reproduction

Working baseline:
- eggs
- constructed or selected nesting sites
- partial egg exposure / burial is plausible
- nest attendance or brooding is plausible
- meaningful parental investment
- juveniles have substantial opportunity to watch older animals

This matters because culture begins with behavior surviving beyond the discoverer.

## Social structure

Working baseline: facultatively social predator.

Expected range:
- solitary foraging/hunting common
- mate and kin tolerance
- parental association
- temporary aggregations
- possible communal nesting or shared nesting grounds
- opportunistic cooperation

Do not portray rigid wolf packs unless later canon deliberately evolves them.

## Founder-stage visual rule

At founder stage, always preserve:
- toothed snout
- feathered body
- long tail
- three-fingered forelimbs
- digitigrade legs
- enlarged second-toe claw

Do not give them:
- human hands
- humanoid torso proportions
- modern bird beaks
- clothing
- civilization markers
- reptilian scales over the whole body

Those features, if they ever appear, must emerge later through evolution or culture.
