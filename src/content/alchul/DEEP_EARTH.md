# Deep Earth

## Important correction

The civilization does not descend while primitive.

By the time sustained migration underground begins, they already have:
- controlled fire
- chemistry
- preservation
- advanced communication
- organized culture
- geological sensing
- Alchul-linked perception
- early boundary science
- habitat engineering

The deep world is transformed by them.

## Initial conditions

Real geology provides:
- karst and solution cavities in suitable carbonate regions
- springs
- groundwater
- fractured rocks
- fault zones
- gas-bearing formations
- geothermal gradients
- chemically powered microbial ecosystems

The earliest deep habitats are fragmented, wet, dark, unstable, and chemically difficult.

## Their intervention

They progressively alter:
- passages
- air
- humidity
- drainage
- water chemistry
- heat
- microbial ecology
- fungal ecology
- food production
- lighting
- gas handling
- structural stability

## Light

There is light below, but the mature light environment is not assumed to have existed at the beginning.

Sources can include:
- cultivated bioluminescent organisms
- luminous microbial/fungal systems
- controlled gas flame
- reflected and redirected light
- mineral-optical structures
- engineered photochemical systems
- later technologies not reducible to present human lighting concepts

Over immense spans, entire habitats become naturally artificial: living ecologies whose illumination is part of the environment itself.

## Ecology

The deep world contains old terrestrial subsurface life and lineages that have developed under isolation.

Some systems may have very ancient ancestry.

Whether any represent truly independent abiogenesis remains unresolved unless later canon specifies it.

The civilization encounters the deep biosphere as another Earth, then progressively integrates with and modifies it.

## Settlement pattern

The descent creates:
- shallow transitional settlements
- route stations
- gas/heat/resource nodes
- ritual/contact chambers
- nurseries
- food and fungal systems
- deeper permanent settlements

Some populations are cut off and diverge.

Some never reach the deepest systems.

Some are born below and have only inherited or mediated knowledge of the sky.

## New Madrid

New Madrid is not merely the place they originated.

It remains one of the civilization's oldest ancestral regions.

The modern seismic zone is linked in-universe to mature deep infrastructure integrated with ancient rift geology.

What humans measure as faulting, pressure, fluid movement, or unusual deep structure may sometimes be the surface expression of systems whose function humans do not recognize.
