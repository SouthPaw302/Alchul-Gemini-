# Alchul

## Status

Alchul is canonically real inside the story and canonically unresolved.

No agent is allowed to define Alchul conclusively.

## What is known

- Alchul is not normally present within the perceptual bandwidth of surface biological life.
- Contact first becomes repeatable through altered states associated with the Harvest.
- Some individuals appear more receptive than others.
- Sensitivity can become partly inherited, trained, culturally cultivated, technologically supported, or all of these.
- Contact can involve dreams, impressions, repeated images, localized temporal perception, shared states, altered attention, intuitions, and later controlled access to the other side.
- Alchul can implant or suggest ideas, but does not simply hand over complete technologies.
- The civilization must experiment, fail, refine, remember, and build.
- Alchul sometimes appears to guide, warn, disturb, or redirect.
- Alchul does not answer the civilization's deepest questions about Alchul.

## Questions that remain open forever unless the user explicitly changes canon

- Is Alchul one being or many?
- Does Alchul have a body?
- Is sex or gender meaningful to Alchul?
- Does Alchul experience time sequentially?
- Is Alchul alive by human definitions?
- Is Alchul native to another dimension, another physical state, another ecology, or something else?
- Did Alchul discover the founders, or were they already part of an ecological relationship?
- Did Alchul save them out of care, need, curiosity, self-interest, or none of these?
- Is Alchul more advanced, or merely native to a layer of reality they were not?

## Writing rule

Alchul should never become a lore-dump device.

Alchul is the mystery at the center of a history that otherwise treats geology, biology, culture, engineering, and civilization as real.
