# Mature Surface Individual — Comparative Biology and 3D Reconstruction Constraints

**Status:** real-world evidence and explicit extrapolation; not fictional canon. Prepared 2026-11-09. Scope: one hypothetical adult of the developed pre-descent civilization, temporarily called **Tesh** in working models. `CANON.md`, `SPECIES.md`, `VISUAL_CANON.md`, `LORE/EMBODIED_WORLDVIEW_ATLAS.md` remain governing constraints.

## Evidence classes

- **Established in sampled animals / fossils:** an actual published observation or peer-reviewed comparative result. It does *not* automatically transfer unchanged to the fictional lineage.
- **Plausible evolutionary extension:** a defensible design hypothesis for an evolved terrestrial theropod-descended lineage.
- **Fictional decision requiring canon approval:** a specific biological, cultural or technological choice for Tesh's group.
- **Unresolved:** cannot presently be inferred and should not be represented by precise measurements.

## 1. Whole-body architecture and center of mass

**Established (general theropod evolution):** Long-tailed non-avian theropods are generally modeled with mass near the hips; across maniraptoran-to-bird evolution, tail, torso and wings changed shape and mass distribution in complex ways. Macaulay et al. (2023) report that terrestrial versus volant lineages predict center-of-mass variation more strongly than a simple 'dinosaur/bird' dichotomy [1].

**Existing story constraint:** A terrestrial, feathered, dromaeosaur-grade founder anatomy is locked. But the peak surface adult is the descendant of extended evolution and culture. No fixed body length, hip height, tail length or exact posture for Tesh is canon.

**Working design:** Prefer a biped whose mass is carried by legs set beneath the pelvis; elongated torso, mobile neck, head held forward or partly raised, tail retained as one of several plausible balance and display structures. The body should read as nonmammalian *before* decoration. Center of mass and support polygon are inferred qualitative cues, **not simulation-derived engineering numbers**.

**Unresolved:** exact allometry; tail mobility/reduction; shoulder girdle and sternum; knee/ankle geometry; inertial parameters. A static model is not a verified gait model.

## 2. Feathers and external covering

**Established (comparative dromaeosaurids):** Forearm quill knobs provide direct evidence that at least one specimen referred to `Velociraptor` supported substantial feathers; broader feather preservation in close theropod relatives supports feathered reconstructions [2]. This does not identify the unnamed Appalachian founder taxon or show a later evolved culture's exact external covering.

**Working design:** Mantle-like regional plumage on neck, torso and proximal limbs, with localized flattened display feathers; contrasting bare functional digit pads and parts of feet. These are **selective evolutionary possibilities**, not fossil-verified Tesh morphology.

**Avoid:** full reptilian scales, mammalian fur, glowing field-aura, a modern bird beak, ornamental robe as substitute for anatomy.

## 3. Hands and work

**Established:** Maniraptorans inherited three principal forelimb digits with joints and wrist adaptations distinct from a primate opposable thumb. The exact grasping capability varies among theropods. Do not use human hand gestures or fully pronated mammal-like palms as default; study the wrist's actual movement constraints.

**Existing story constraint:** The lineage eventually develops powerful fine manipulation by adaptation of an ancestral three-fingered forelimb—not by copying a human hand.

**Working design:** A three-contact grip with two digits bracing a tool or resonant stave and one guiding/aligning it. Show articulated fingers rather than feather-wings used like mittens. The extent of individual digital opposition, pronation, palm pad and keratin at peak remains unsettled.

**Unresolved:** exact tendon paths, joint ranges, fingertip receptor types, force envelopes, hand size.

## 4. Breath and respiratory ecology

**Established (theropod evidence):** Skeletal pneumatization is strong comparative evidence for air-sac structures in some nonavian theropods, with variation by clade [3, 4]. Rib/uncinate process analyses support a ventilation system with birdlike mechanical features in some maniraptorans [5]. However, specific airflow direction in extinct taxa is difficult to infer directly from bone morphology, and cannot be assigned automatically [3].

**Existing story constraint:** Egg-bearing individuals retain embryos internally through ~75% of embryonic development before final external incubation.

**Established (reptile analogue):** Reptilian egg retention faces oxygen/gas-exchange constraints; altered eggshell timing and properties can be part of evolutionary accommodation [6]. Specific mechanisms in the fictional lineage remain unknown.

**Working design:** A mature active lineage with sophisticated respiratory regulation is plausible, especially as ventilation and egg-development ecology become important, but no exact number of air sacs, lung flows, oxygen threshold, organ geometry or thermal setpoint can be drawn. In the 3D model, chest motion signals **breathing as a general behavior**, not the unseen mechanism.

**Avoid:** transparent fictional air sacs labeled factual anatomy; an exact gestational pouch, mammalian pregnancy, a fixed clutch size or percentage beyond the canon ~75%.

## 5. Sensing space and substrate

**Established comparative biology:** Crocodilians possess highly responsive facial and integumentary mechanosensory organs, with some units responding to small mechanical forces and low-frequency vibration [7]. That is evidence that archosaurs can evolve extraordinary mechanosensation, **not** evidence the same organ occurs in maniraptorans.

**Working design:** Tesh uses contact through foot pads and hand bracing, plus evolved visual, acoustic, scent and environmental sensitivity; residents learn to integrate weak cues with instruments and records. Sparse sensory filament forms on neck/jaw are *visual placeholders*, not confirmed organs.

**Important separation:** Detecting real physical changes in stress, flow or vibration is not the same as Alchul-associated perception of a location at another time. Some environmental inference may look uncanny but is not supernatural; anomalous contact remains a distinct unresolved phenomenon.

## 6. Voice, expression and mutual attention

**Established comparative biology:** Closed-mouth acoustic signals have evolved independently many times among birds. Some researchers argue they may also have been available to certain extinct nonavian dinosaurs, but exact sound-producing organs and repertoire are not recoverable from this alone [8].

**Existing canon:** Multimodal communication may include acoustic, posture, filament/feather, touch, chemical and substrate patterns.

**Working design:** Use body orientation, movement interruption, tail posture, feather mantle tilt, timed contact and a limited nonhuman signal rhythm. No human lips or conventional smiles. Text labels are translations, not transcript evidence.

**Unresolved:** spoken phonology, grammar, syntax, sexual displays, individual names and cultural norms.

## 7. Reproduction, identity and social roles

**Canon:** ~75% internal egg development at the peak surface stage. Egg-bearing adults exist. Exact sex/gender, reproductive anatomy, clutch size, lifespan, social parenthood, and infant capacities are still undecided.

**Working design:** **Tesh's reproductive status is unspecified**. Tesh participates in protecting dependent young, but care is a social task and cannot be assigned automatically to a sex. A nursery scene is about safe air, movement and shared resources, not a claim that Tesh has gestated eggs.

## 8. The study model is *parametric sculpture*, not a fossil scan

Any mesh built in `src/components/MatureSurfaceBodyLab.tsx` uses **arbitrary scene units** for visual proportion and interaction—not meters, kilograms, gait validation, published joint measurements or a reconstructed fossil specimen.

A viewer should be able to:
- orbit and inspect body from side, above and front;
- turn anatomical system labels on and off;
- switch among several *task poses* (reading water, precision material handling, protecting a route, negotiating a report);
- understand work, care and disagreement as dynamic life rather than a statue;
- read an explicit **confidence tag** for inherited anatomy vs proposed evolved traits;
- see no literal representation of the body, plans or wishes of Alchul.

## 9. Research matrix

| System | What the model may show | What is *not yet known* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theropod locomotor descent | Bipedal hip-centered support, nonhuman digitigrade legs | Exact future gait, size, support mechanics |
| Tail | Balance and display candidate | Whether peak lineage still needs a long tail |
| Manipulative forelimb | Three distinct functional digits | Joint opposition, grip forces, tendon anatomy |
| Cranial sensory focus | Nonhuman jaws and forward attention | Confirmed neurocranium dimensions, visual field |
| Integument | Feather-derived covering plausible | Exact distribution, pattern, receptor morphogenesis |
| Respiratory motion | Chest expansion as a generic animation | Air sac network, flow direction, oxygen efficiency |
| Egg retention | Late internal development and final external incubation | Mechanism, clutch, shell, incubation temperature |
| Social behavior | Body posture and spatial boundaries | Native words, kin structure, reproductive gender system |
| Alchul contact | Report of uncertain perception | Alchul ontology, shape, cognition, agenda |
